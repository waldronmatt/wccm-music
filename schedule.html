Exploring the Classics Part I

November 24th - 26th (Monday - Saturday) 11:00 - 12:00pm

Don't miss this week's edition of Exploring The Classics (w/ Diane Wells) where she'll be discussing the Life of Franz Liszt. Local Talent featuring "The Perfect Fifths"

Friday Novemeber 28th @ 6:00 - 7:00pm

We are happy to announce the forth WCCM radio performance of The Perfect Fifths. Founded by Julio Estrada and Chelsea Hall, they are an upcoming neo classical group with Latin-styled percussive elements, intricate piano and string segements, and melodic indie styled guitar licks. Over the past year, The Perfect Fifths have catipulted into the music scene of CT and came in Second Place in Battle of The Bands in Hartford, CT 2014. Their music has captivated the minds of many and on Friday, they will be playing their new song "August Breeze" of their new album "Salt of the Earth" for the very first time.

Fiddler on the Roof

Sunday November 30th @ 1:00 - 3:00pm

This Sunday will be our first public broadcast of Fiddler on the Roof live from the Thomaston Opera House. Many notable actors include Joshua Walters (Plays as Tevye), Melinda Thornton, and Morris Brown (Plays as Perchik) who have been involved in over 12 musicals and plays at the Thomaston Opera House. A big thank you to all three of them for their dedication and hard work over the years. Our newest actor, Angel Ward, will be having her debut performace in her very first musical! We very excited for her success and wish her the very best in future musical endeavors.

Special Guest Appearance on Sunday Evening Sonatas

Sunday November 30th @ 6:00 - 7:00pm
